Fall Into... Book Binding

Description

Join us this October 10th and learn the beautiful craft of bookbinding. Make your own custom hardcover journal while enjoying a boba tea on a relaxing October evening. This class is for anyone interested in learning the art of bookbinding and does not require any previous experience. We will go over the basics, and you will walk away with a fully intact book that you can use and the knowledge to make it again. We are excited to see you all there! 😊
