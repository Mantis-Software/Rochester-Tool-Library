About Our Organization

Rochester Tool Library was created in 2026 to spread creativity. One can't escape the increase of prices, but this should not hinder the pursuit of creative endeavors for anyone. We wanted to create an organization that not only shared the spirit of creativity, but also provided the resources for people to pursue all creative ventures.

About The Founder

Hello, My name is Mario and I founded Rochester Tool Library to provide resources and inspiration for anyone and everyone. I personally love to create in many mediums like caligraphy, writing, wood working, leather working, electronics, programming, 3D printing, drawing, and many more. I find creativity is important for the human soul, especially in a heavily artificial world. Tools are expensive and I simply want to share the tools to share creativty amongst all who want to create.
